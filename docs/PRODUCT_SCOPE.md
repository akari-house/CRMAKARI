# Product Scope

## Product identity

AKARI CRM is the product. AKARI House is Customer 001 and the first real organisation workspace.

Future projects and companies receive separate tenant workspaces. They do not receive access to AKARI House data.

## First customer workflow

- Manage 542 imported projects and leads
- Assign ownership and follow-up dates
- Track multiple opportunities per project
- Convert won opportunities to campaigns
- Track campaign delivery, revenue, costs and referral rewards
- Track payment schedules and outstanding balances
- Show monthly targets, daily scorecards and business attention alerts

## Future platform workflow

A platform administrator can create an independent organisation, set its owner, plan, user limit, modules, currency and timezone. Each organisation manages its own team and CRM configuration.
