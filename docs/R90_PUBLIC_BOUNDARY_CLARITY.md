# R90 — CRM public boundary clarity

CRM by AKARI is a separate product from AKARI House.

Public and protected CRM surfaces must identify the product as **CRM by AKARI** and avoid wording that makes the CRM appear to be the AKARI House member application.

## Copy rules

- Use `CRM by AKARI` or `AKARI CRM` when naming this product.
- Use `CRM login` or `Enter CRM by AKARI`, not generic `AKARI login`.
- Illustrative CRM workspace previews must be labelled as CRM workspaces, not `AKARI House` product screens.
- Do not describe AKARI House as `Customer 001` in user-facing copy.
- Sharing the AKARI brand mark is allowed; product purpose and interface identity must stay distinct.

## Product boundary

AKARI House owns the professional network, member profiles, projects, campaigns, Investor opportunities and events.

CRM by AKARI owns leads, contacts, relationship operations, follow-ups, agreements, finance and revenue operations.

Server-to-server integration may exist through reviewed API contracts without merging the two product experiences.
